The Death of Tobias Renn

Step 1 · Cause

He was asphyxiated. Not poisoned, not struck, not frightened into a heart attack by a bad diagnosis he already had. The oxygen around him was taken away.

Evidence: B-1 — hypoxic asphyxia, toxicology negative throughout, airway clear, no trauma.

Step 2 · The file is wrong about when

Everything the original investigation believed rests on one number, and that number was measured in the wrong room.

Paramedics carried Tobias Renn out of Cold Store 3 into the B2 corridor at 00:11, to try to resuscitate him somewhere he could be resuscitated. At 00:29, eighteen minutes later, the responding officer took the scene temperature. He took it where he was standing. 20.8 °C — B2 corridor. It went onto the intake form. It went to the Medical Examiner. The Medical Examiner did the arithmetic perfectly.

But Renn did not die in the corridor. He died in a store held at 6.0 °C, continuously, all evening, because the Halvorsen loan agreement says albumen prints are kept at six degrees.

Liver temperature 32.5 °C at 00:41 — a fall of 4.5 °C.

At the reference constant: 4.5 ÷ 0.9 = 5.0 hours → 19:41. This is what the file says.

At 6 °C the multiplier is 1.94 (B-1, Appendix M).

5.0 ÷ 1.94 = 2.6 hours → 22:06.

Tobias Renn died at about ten past ten at night, not twenty to eight. And he told you so himself: at 21:51:07 he saved a file. Two hours after the Medical Examiner has him dead, he was sitting at workstation B2-CONS-02, typing.

Evidence: B-1 (methodology + Appendix M), A-1 (reading taken in the corridor, after the body was moved), D-1 §2 (6.0 °C, continuous), D-1 §4 (21:51:07).

Step 3 · Mechanism

At 21:58:41 somebody stood at panel B2-P1, in the corridor outside Cold Store 3, and dumped the oxygen in that room to nine per cent by hand.

The system said there was a person inside. It said so out loud, in the log: INTERLOCK — OCCUPANCY DETECTED IN ZONE. Overriding that is not a slip. You present a Level-4 credential and you hold the override while the discharge commits. It cannot be done remotely. It cannot be done in error.

Twenty-two minutes later, at 22:20:13, the same panel vented the room. Somebody waited outside a sealed door for twenty-two minutes and then opened it.

Evidence: SLIP 11 — full life-safety event log, both events, interlock override, oxygen trace 15.0% → 9.4% by 22:06.

She did not come in through the lobby.

At 21:32:18 credential 0003 — the Facility Director's — opened the Level D service door. At 21:34:52 it called the freight elevator. At 21:35:19 the car reached Vault Level B2. At 22:30:41 it was called again, from B2. At 22:34:51 the same credential left by the same service door.

Those are the only two movements the freight car made all night, and both belong to her.

Step 6 · The alibi

She said she was at the Perrin Foundation dinner until a quarter past ten.

The last photograph of her at the Athenaeum is timestamped 20:16. Her table's terminal settled at 20:20. Her guest card was never used again. Forty guests in a floating room, and not one of them, and not one member of the venue staff, can put her anywhere at any specific moment after twenty past eight.

The Athenaeum is twenty-six minutes from Halligan Wharf. She had seventy-two.

Evidence: SLIP 08, against her own account at C-1.

Step 7 · The cleared

Danny Ferraro was on a boat. 190 km, no return sailing before 01:20. (E-1.2)

Dr Lena Kovaleski badged out of B2 at 21:26:44, was on camera in the lobby at 21:33, and validated a transit card 6.2 km away at 21:35. Renn died at about 22:06 and the discharge fired at 21:58. Against the time of death printed in the file, none of that clears her at all — which is exactly why the file never looked past her. (D-1 §1, SLIP 09)

What Pryce was actually hiding: he was asleep off-post in the loading bay office, never walked the 22:00 round, and wrote it into the book afterwards. Then he found a body and spent fourteen minutes fixing his paperwork before he rang 999.

$412,000, in three payments, from a numbered account in Vaduz.

Four albumen prints — HB-021, HB-022, HB-030, HB-031 — sold by private treaty and replaced with modern gelatin-silver reproductions toned to look like albumen. Unlawful Killing

VIVIENNE MARCHETTI

What happened

He messaged her at 18:41 and she read it two minutes later, in the car, on her way to a room full of donors. Found the problem with Halvorsen. Not damage. She went to the dinner. She was photographed nine times. She left somewhere after twenty past eight, when forty people were standing up and moving around and nobody was looking at anybody, and she drove twenty-two kilometres and let herself in at the loading dock.

He was still working. At ten to ten he saved the fourth draft of a document that named four accession numbers and recommended notifying the insurers, and he did not send it, because he was going to send it in the morning.

She went in. Whatever was said took about three minutes. Then she stepped into the corridor, closed a door that seals, and stood at a panel that told her in plain text there was a person in the room — and she held the override down anyway.

Then she waited twenty-two minutes in an empty corridor, vented the room, went back in, and took his tablet, because the audit was on the tablet.

The audit was not on the tablet. It was on the server, saved at 21:51:07, ten minutes before she got there.

And the room she chose — the only room in the building cold enough to hold a nineteenth-century photograph — moved his death two and a half hours into a stretch of the evening she could prove she was somewhere else. She did not plan that. She got it for nothing, and it very nearly held.

It was undone by a police constable writing down the temperature of the wrong room.
