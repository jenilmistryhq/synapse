Responding Officer Narrative — PC 4471 D. Aumont

On arrival I was met by the caller, PRYCE, who was alone in the building and appeared agitated. He conducted us to Vault Level B2 by the passenger lift.

Cold Store 3 is a sealed climate-controlled room off the B2 corridor. The door was standing open on our arrival and the store's climate panel was showing FAULT — DOOR OPEN. Signage on the door reads: REDUCED OXYGEN ATMOSPHERE · 15% O₂ NOMINAL · MAXIMUM EXPOSURE 30 MINUTES · PERSONAL MONITOR REQUIRED.

An adult male was lying prone in the centre of the store, approximately two metres inside the threshold, between two rows of flat storage cabinets. No signs of struggle. Nothing overturned. No blood. The deceased's spectacles were folded in his breast pocket.

At 00:11 paramedics relocated the male to the B2 corridor in order to attempt resuscitation in a breathable atmosphere. Resuscitation was attempted and discontinued. Life was pronounced extinct at 00:18 by AMB-27.

The male was identified by PRYCE and by the staff photograph on his lanyard as RENN, Tobias, 54, Head of Conservation.

B2 is badge-controlled. There is no sign of forced entry to the level, the store, or the building. A personal oxygen monitor of the type required by the door signage was found seated in its charging cradle in the B2 anteroom, fully charged and not deployed.

Appendix M — Cooling Rate: Methodological Note

Post-mortem cooling is not constant. The rate of heat loss is proportional to the differential between body temperature and the temperature of the surrounding environment. This Office applies a standard constant of 0.9 °C/hr at a reference ambient of 21 °C.

Where the ambient temperature at the place of death departs materially from that reference, the constant must be scaled before use:

rate = 0.9 × (37 − ambient) ÷ (37 − 21)

Note to investigating officers

The elapsed interval scales inversely with the multiplier. An interval computed at the reference constant may be converted to any other ambient by dividing by that ambient's multiplier. The ambient figure supplied to this Office is taken from the scene intake form and is not independently verified by the examiner. Where the location of the body differs from the location of the temperature reading, the interval above must be recomputed.
