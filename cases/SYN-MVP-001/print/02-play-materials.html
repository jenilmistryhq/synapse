Case Review Unit · Terms of Reference

Case Review — Renn, Tobias (Deceased)

On the morning of 7 March, Tobias Renn was found dead in a refrigerated art store beneath a commercial warehouse on Halligan Wharf. He was 54. He had a heart condition, a written medical exemption barring him from that room, and he had gone into it anyway without his monitor, as he had done perhaps a hundred times before.

The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner recorded acute hypoxic asphyxia and a provisional manner of accidental. The file was closed in eleven days.

Six weeks later the estate's insurers declined the claim on the Halvorsen Bequest and wrote to this force. The file has been reopened for review.

Your task

You are the review team. You have the closed file — everything the original investigation gathered, which is not everything that exists.

Determine whether Tobias Renn's death was an accident. If it was not, say who caused it, and show your working.

How this works

1 · The file is open to all of you

Every detective may read every page of the base dossier — A-1 through E-1. Nothing is hidden from anyone at this table. Spread it out. Read out loud. The documents do not lie to you.

2 · The witnesses might

The transcripts (C-1 to C-4) are people talking. People misremember, minimise, and occasionally lie outright. Every untruth in this case can be caught by a document. If a statement and a log disagree, the log is right.

3 · You have a limited number of authorities

Not every record was pulled the first time round. Eleven further lines of inquiry are open to you and are listed on the next page. Each one you pursue costs one Authority. You have a fixed number and they do not come back.

DetectivesAuthorities

To spend one, say the number out loud, agree it as a table, then open the matching sealed slip. Cross it off the menu. Not every authority returns something useful. Choosing well is the game.

4 · Fill in the Resolution Sheet as you go

The Case Resolution Sheet is your working. Write evidence references (EV or document numbers) into it as you establish each step, before you accuse. Anything not written down before the accusation scores nothing — a right answer for the wrong reason is not police work.

5 · Accusing

You may make two accusations that turn out to be wrong. Each costs you points and returns a Reconsider slip telling you what your theory has failed to account for. Your third accusation is final and is scored as it stands.

When you are ready, complete the sheet, name your suspect, and only then open ENVELOPE S-1.

One rule above the others

Do not open Envelope S-1 early. There is no version of this evening that is improved by it.
