# PRINT GUIDE — SYN-MVP-001 "The Halvorsen Bequest"

Everything you need to put this case on a table. **Minimum 33 sheets.**

## PRINTER SETTINGS

Open each `.html` in **Chrome or Edge** → `Ctrl+P`, then:

| Setting | Value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Paper size | **A4** | The layout is built for it. |
| Margins | **Default** | The CSS sets its own; "None" will clip the classification bars. |
| Scale | **100%** (not "Fit to page") | Keeps the log tables aligned. |
| **Headers and footers** | **OFF** | ⚠️ Otherwise every page prints a URL and a date across the top and the documents stop looking real. This is the one setting people forget. |
| **Background graphics** | **ON** | ⚠️ Off, and the tinted boxes and rules vanish — including the shaded box on B-1 p2 that carries the cooling formula. |
| Colour | **Greyscale / Black & white** | Everything is monochrome by design. Saves your colour cartridge. |

Do a **single test page first** (print just page 1 of file 01) and check that the black rule under
"ALDENMOOR METROPOLITAN POLICE" reaches both margins. If it doesn't, your scale isn't 100%.

| 4 | **C-1** Interview: V. Marchetti |
| 5 | **C-2** Interview: O. Pryce |
| 6 | **C-3** Interview: Dr L. Kovaleski |
| 7 | **C-4** Interview: D. Ferraro |
| 8 | **D-1** Facility Systems, page 1 — *access control + climate log* |
| 9 | **D-1** Facility Systems, page 2 — *life-safety status + server log* |
| 10 | **E-1** Records & Exhibits |

### Extra copies — worth the paper

Three pages get physically fought over, because three or four people all want them at the same
moment. Print **one spare of each**:

> **Pages 3, 8 and 9.**

That is B-1 page 2 (the cooling table everyone needs a pencil against) and both pages of D-1 (every
timestamp in the case). Three extra sheets removes the single most common table friction.

| Detectives | Base dossier sheets |
|---|---|
| 1 (solo) | 10 — no spares needed |
| 2–3 | 13 (10 + spares of pp. 3, 8, 9) |
| 4–6 | 16 (10 + **two** spares each of pp. 3, 8, 9) |

**Single-sided is strongly recommended.** The whole design assumes ten documents lying open at once
so people can point at two of them simultaneously. Double-siding halves your paper and roughly
doubles your arguments about who has D-1.

*(What each returns is deliberately not listed here. The number on the page is all you need.)*

**Separating them.** Collect the stack face-down as it comes out of the printer. Then either:

- **Envelopes** — eleven of them, write 01 to 11 on the front, one page in each. Best.

  outside, one staple. Works fine, costs nothing.

Do not paperclip them into a stack. Someone will fan it.

## 4 · RECONSIDER & ENVELOPE S-1 — `04-envelope-s1.html` ⚠️ SPOILERS

**Print pages 1–8.** These are two different things and they get sealed differently.

### Pages 1–3 — Reconsider inserts · **single-sided, three separate envelopes**

Opened **during** play, when the table accuses the wrong person.

| Page | Envelope label |
|---|---|
| 1 | RECONSIDER — **PRYCE** |
| 2 | RECONSIDER — **KOVALESKI** |
| 3 | RECONSIDER — **FERRARO** |

If a group accuses correctly on the first try, all three stay sealed. That's the good outcome, not
wasted paper.

### Pages 4–8 — Envelope S-1 · **one envelope, all five together**

Opened **once**, at the very end, after the Resolution Sheet is signed. Double-sided is fine here —
these pages are read in sequence, not spread out.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 4 | S-1 cover — the "do not open" page. Put this one facing outward. |
| 5 | Resolution, page 1 — cause, the time-of-death correction, mechanism |
| 6 | Resolution, page 2 — means, opportunity, the alibi, the cleared |
| 7 | Resolution, page 3 — motive, verdict, what actually happened |
| 8 | Scoring sheet |

Seal it properly. A gummed envelope, or fold and tape the edge. The single largest threat to this
case is somebody idly turning it over an hour before the end.

## TOTALS

| Setup | Sheets | Envelopes |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | 33 | 15 |
| **2–3 detectives** | 36 | 15 |
| **4–6 detectives** | 39–40 | 15 |

**Envelopes: 15.** Eleven numbered 01–11, three marked RECONSIDER (Pryce / Kovaleski / Ferraro), one
marked **S-1**. Standard DL or C5 both work; C5 lets you keep the page unfolded.

### Minimum-ink variant — 26 sheets

If paper or toner is genuinely tight:

- Print file **01 double-sided** → 5 sheets instead of 10, and skip the spares.
- Print file **02 double-sided** → 2 sheets.
- Files **03 and 04 pages 1–3 must stay single-sided.** No way around it; they get separated.
- File **04 pages 4–8 double-sided** → 3 sheets.

Total 26 sheets. The game still works. You will hear more of *"can you pass me D-1"* than you would
like.
