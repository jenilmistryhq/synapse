# PRINT GUIDE - SYN-MVP-003 "The Ashcombe Dome"

The Expert case. **About 41 A4 sheets, 16 envelopes, two to three hours.**

## At a glance

| File | Pages | Copies | Who may handle it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-base-dossier.html` | 1-11 | 1 | Anyone. No spoilers. |
| `02-play-materials.html` | 1-4 | 1 per table | Anyone. No spoilers. |
| `03-action-results.html` | 1-10 | 1 | Printer only. |
| `04-envelope-s1.html` | 1-10 | 1 | Printer only. |

If you are playing, do not print files 3 and 4 yourself. Ask someone else, or print them
face-down and put them in envelopes without looking.

## Printer settings

Open each file in Chrome or Edge, press `Ctrl+P`, and choose **A4**, **Default** margins and
**100%** scale. Or use the ready-made PDFs, or the zip, in `pdf/`.

## Envelopes

1. Ten envelopes numbered **01** to **10**: one Authority result page in each.
2. Five envelopes marked **RECONSIDER**, one each for pages 1 to 5 of file 4: One name, Brackley,
   Okafor, E. Hale, Nowak.
3. One envelope marked **S-1**: pages 6 to 10 of file 4, together, cover facing out.

`pdf/envelope-labels.pdf` has a label for every envelope.

## Playing

The file opens in three phases: read what the memo names, write down your theory, then open the
next bundle. Solo and pairs get **5 Authorities**; three or more players get 6. The final
accusation may need two names.
