Case Review Unit · Terms of Reference

Case Review - Hale, Edmund (Deceased)

On the morning of 7 January the director of the Ashcombe Observatory was found dead at the foot of a ladder, inside a telescope dome that had been bolted from the inside. The caretaker forced the door. The file was closed as an accidental fall.

His sister does not believe it. Neither, on paper, does the pathologist. The coroner has allowed a review.

Your task

Decide whether Edmund Hale's death was an accident. If it was not, name everyone responsible, and show your working.

This is an Expert file. Not every document in it is what it says it is.

How this works

1 · The file opens in three phases

Read what the memo names, write down your theory, then open the next bundle.

2 · Logs don't lie. People might. So might a printout.

If a statement and a log disagree, the log is right. But a log is only as honest as the person who printed it. Ask who supplied each record, and whether it is complete.

3 · Five Authorities

Ten lines of inquiry are open. Solo and pairs get five; three or more players get six. Several of them return nothing you need.

4 · Write it down as you go

Every step on the Case Resolution Sheet needs a citation. Your final finding may need more than one name.

5 · Accusing

Two wrong accusations are allowed. Each costs 10 points and returns a Reconsider slip. The third is final. Then open ENVELOPE S-1.
