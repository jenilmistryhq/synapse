Case Review - The Tennant Watch

A gold pocket watch worth $4,800 went missing overnight from a small watchmaker's shop on Quay Street. The investigating officer suspected the apprentice, could not prove it, and closed the file.

The watch belongs to Mr Alasdair Tennant. It was his grandfather's. He has complained that nobody looked properly, and the file has come to the Case Review Unit. It is your first.

Your task

You have the closed file: everything the first officer gathered, which is not everything that exists.

Work out when the watch went, and who took it. Show your working.

How this works

1 · The file is open to you

Read every page of the base dossier, A-1 to E-1. Nothing is hidden from you.

2 · Logs don't lie. People might.

The statements (C-1 to C-3) are people talking. If a statement and a log disagree, the log is right. Every untruth in this file can be caught by a document.

3 · You have a few Authorities

Six further lines of inquiry are listed on the next page. Each one costs one Authority, and you have three. Not every one returns something useful. Spend them on what the file points at.

4 · Write it down as you go

The Case Resolution Sheet is your working. Answer each step with the document that proves it. A step with no citation scores nothing.

5 · Accusing

You may be wrong twice. Each wrong accusation costs points and returns a Reconsider slip telling you what your theory misses. The third accusation is final. Then open ENVELOPE S-1.
