# PRINT GUIDE - SYN-MVP-000 "The Consignment Watch"

The training case. **22 A4 sheets, 9 envelopes, about 20 minutes.**

## At a glance

| File | Pages | Copies | Who may handle it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-base-dossier.html` | 1-6 | 1 | Anyone. No spoilers. |
| `02-play-materials.html` | 1-4 | 1 per table | Anyone. No spoilers. |
| `03-action-results.html` | 1-6 | 1 | Printer only. |
| `04-envelope-s1.html` | 1-6 | 1 | Printer only. |

If you are playing, do not print files 3 and 4 yourself. Ask someone else, or print them
face-down and put them in envelopes without looking.

## Printer settings

Open each file in Chrome or Edge, press `Ctrl+P`, and choose **A4**, **Default** margins and
**100%** scale. Or use the ready-made PDFs in `pdf/`.

## Envelopes

1. Six envelopes numbered **01** to **06**: one Authority result page in each.
2. Two envelopes marked **RECONSIDER - LARK** and **RECONSIDER - HOLLIS**: pages 1 and 2 of file 4.
3. One envelope marked **S-1**: pages 3 to 6 of file 4, together, cover facing out.

`pdf/envelope-labels.pdf` has a label for every envelope.

## Playing

Read the briefing aloud. Each player gets **3 Authorities** (4 for 3 or more players). Write on
the Case Resolution Sheet as you go, accuse when you are sure, then open S-1.
