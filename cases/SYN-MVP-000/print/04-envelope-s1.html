The Tennant Watch

Step 1 · Last seen

The watch was last seen at about five to five on Friday, when Mr Voss handed it back across the counter.

Evidence: C-1 (Priya Desai's own account), D-1 §4 (the 16:40 viewing).

Step 2 · Not at night

Theo Lark's night visit is real, and it is irrelevant.

The safe records every open and every close. After 17:57:40 on Friday it did not open again until Mrs Hollis opened it at 08:34:10 on Saturday. And Theo has no safe code at all. Whatever he did at 22:14, he could not have opened that safe, and the safe says nobody did.

Evidence: D-1 §2 (the safe log), D-1 §3 (staff codes), C-2.

Step 3 · The open safe

The safe opened at 16:38 for the viewing and did not close until 17:57.

For an hour and nineteen minutes the safe stood open. From 17:30, when Mrs Hollis left for the dentist, one person was in the shop with it: Priya Desai.

Evidence: D-1 §2 (open 16:38:12, close 17:57:40), E-1.1 (the rota: one member of staff after 17:30).

Step 4 · The account that fails

She said she put the watch straight back in the safe and closed it the minute Mr Voss left. The safe says otherwise.

The counter camera shows the rest. At 17:49 she wrapped the watch in tissue and put it in her own bag. At 17:57 she put the empty tray in the safe and shut the door, so that in the morning it would look as if the watch had vanished from inside a locked safe.

Evidence: C-1 against D-1 §2, and SLIP 03.

PRIYA DESAI

What happened

She opened the safe for a viewing and simply did not close it. With the owner gone and the apprentice at college, she had the shop to herself for half an hour. She took the watch, shut the empty tray in the safe, and set the alarm with her own code.

Then Theo went back for his charger, and the alarm log handed the first officer a suspect. She did not plan that part. She made sure to mention that he was short of money.

The safe told the truth all along. Nobody asked it.
