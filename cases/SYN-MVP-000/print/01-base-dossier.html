Crime ref25-11-0302
Reported08:36 SAT 15 NOV
OffenceTHEFT FROM A SHOP
PremisesHOLLIS & DAUGHTER
Address14 QUAY STREET
OfficerDC S. NAIRN

Property Stolen

One gold open-face pocket watch, "the Tennant watch". Engraved inside the case back J.T. 1911. Held by the shop on consignment for the owner, Mr Alasdair Tennant, for sale at an asking price of $4,800.

Report

At 08:34 on Saturday 15 November the owner, Mrs Margaret HOLLIS, opened the display safe to set out the morning trays. The velvet tray that holds the Tennant watch was in its place on the top shelf of the safe. The watch was not on it. She telephoned police at 08:36.

There is no sign of forced entry to the shop, the workshop or the safe. The shop alarm was set when Mrs Hollis arrived and she disarmed it with her own code.

The shop alarm keypad records which staff code is used. Its log shows that the alarm was unset at 22:14 on Friday night with the code belonging to the apprentice, Theo LARK, and set again five minutes later. Mr Lark accepts that he went back to the shop. He says he went only to collect his phone charger from the workshop.

Investigating Officer's Summary

Mr Lark is the only person known to have entered the premises between closing on Friday and opening on Saturday. He had the opportunity. He is 19, he is paid an apprentice's wage, and he gave no satisfactory reason why a phone charger could not wait until Monday.

The watch has not been recovered and there is no direct evidence connecting Mr Lark to it. Insufficient evidence to charge. No further action. Suspect: LARK, Theo.

Filed. Complaint received from the consignor, Mr Tennant, on 24 November: he does not accept that the matter was properly investigated. Referred to the Case Review Unit.
