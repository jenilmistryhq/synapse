Referral — Ravensgate, 19 November

SiteRAVENSGATE DAM
OperatorCAIRNHOLD POWER
Deaths2
Police findingMURDER–SUICIDE
RDSI positionDISSENT
AuthorCHIEF INSPECTOR R. HALSEY

The Inspectorate does not investigate homicide and does not presume to. We investigate why plant kills people. On this occasion the two enquiries have produced findings that cannot both be correct, and the disagreement is not a matter of judgement. It is a matter of water.

The police investigation concluded that Ines Ballard killed Aurelio Sarn at 06:03 and then entered the forebay and drowned herself. Her body was recovered at 07:25 from trash rack panel 4, inside the intake gallery, at elevation 218.4 m.

The point at issue

The intake gallery is not open water. It is a sealed concrete structure downstream of intake gate 2. It is dry above the sill whenever that gate is shut, and intake gate 2 was shut from 03:12:44 that morning until 08:40:19.

The police report does not address how the deceased came to be inside it. It records where she was found and does not ask how she got there. We are unable to identify any route by which a body could enter the intake gallery after 03:12.

We make no allegation and offer no alternative account. We say only that a finding which places a living woman at a switchgear cabinet at 06:02 must also explain how her body reached a location that was, by then, unreachable.

The Inspectorate's technical file is provided in full. Our own conduct in respect of the 2019 instrumentation renewal at this site is also within the scope of this review, and we have not redacted it.

SignedR. HALSEY, CHIEF INSPECTOR

Matters Not Resolved

Recorded here for completeness at the direction of the SIO.

  • No note or communication of intent was recovered from Ms Ballard.
  • Ms Ballard's credential card was not on her person when recovered. Its location is recorded in the property schedule and was not thought material.
  • The Office of the Chief Medical Examiner was unable to estimate a time of death for Ms Ballard. The investigation proceeded on the basis that she died after Mr Sarn, that being the sequence the credential evidence implies.
  • No motive was established. Professional friction between the deceased was reported by two witnesses and denied by two others.

Note by the Case Review Unit. The third bullet above is the whole of the reason this file is in front of you. The sequence of the two deaths was not established by evidence. It was inferred from the finding, and the finding was then built on the sequence.

Post-Mortem — SARN, Aurelio

CaseOCME 24-2871
Age / Sex58 / M
ExaminerDR H. ADEYEMI

External Examination

Full-thickness contact burn to the right palm, 34 mm, with central charring and a raised margin — a classic electrical entry wound. Corresponding exit wound to the left forearm, 21 mm, at the point of contact with an earthed steel cabinet frame. Singeing of the forearm hair. No other injury of any kind. No defensive wounds. Nothing to suggest a struggle, and nothing to suggest restraint.

Internal Examination

Findings consistent with a hand-to-hand current path across the thorax — the configuration that reliably arrests the heart. Contraction-band necrosis of the myocardium. No pre-existing cardiac disease of significance. No other contributory pathology.

Toxicology

Negative throughout. No alcohol, no drugs of abuse, no prescribed medication above therapeutic range, no carbon monoxide.

Cause of Death

Ia. ELECTROCUTION — consistent with contact with an 11 kV conductor.

MANNER: UNLAWFUL KILLING. Death was effectively instantaneous.

Time of Death

06:03, 19 November. This is not a pathological estimate and does not need to be. The earth-fault alarm cascade at the station is timestamped 06:03, the deceased was found at 06:10, and the body was warm and unfixed on examination at the scene. The time of this death is not in dispute and never has been.

Autopsy Protocol · Decedent 2

Post-Mortem — BALLARD, Ines

CaseOCME 24-2872
Age / Sex41 / F
ExaminerDR H. ADEYEMI

External Examination

Post-mortem abrasion and contusion to the anterior trunk, shoulders and knees, distributed in the pattern produced by prolonged contact with a coarse steel grating under flow. No injury shows a vital reaction — all of it occurred after death.

No defensive injuries. No grip marks to the arms. No ligature. No injury to the back of the head or neck. Fingernails intact and clean.

Internal Examination

Frothy oedema fluid in the airway. Over-inflated lungs with rib impressions. Water in the stomach and duodenum. Diatom recovery from bone marrow matches the forebay profile and not the tailrace. She was alive when she entered the water and she entered it upstream.

Cause of Death

Ia. DROWNING — freshwater, cold.

MANNER: OPEN. The pathology cannot distinguish between entering the water voluntarily, falling, and being put in. There is nothing on this body that decides it.

Time of Death — Statement of Limitation

Read this before relying on any estimate

The decedent was immersed in moving water at 3–5 °C for an unknown but substantial period. This invalidates, individually and together:

Algor mortisBody temperature equilibrated with the water long before recovery. The differential carries no information.
Rigor mortisCold profoundly retards onset and the body was additionally subject to continuous mechanical loading against the rack.
Livor mortisPosition was not fixed and flow was continuous. No usable pattern.
Vitreous potassiumUnreliable below 10 °C ambient. This Office does not report it in immersion cases.

THIS OFFICE CANNOT ESTIMATE A TIME OF DEATH FOR THIS DECEDENT AND DECLINES TO ATTEMPT ONE. Any sequence of the two deaths must be established from evidence other than pathology. It cannot be established from this report, and this report should not be cited as if it had been.
