Ravensgate

Step 1 · Two deaths, and only one of them has a clock

Aurelio Sarn died at 06:03 and nobody has ever doubted it. Ines Ballard drowned, and the Medical Examiner told you in capital letters that no pathologist can say when.

Everything the original investigation concluded followed from filling that gap with an assumption. It assumed she died second, because the credential evidence implied she did — and then used the sequence to prove the credential evidence. The file says so itself, in the third bullet of A-1, and nobody read it.

Evidence: B-1, B-2, A-1.

Step 2 · The water is the clock

She was found at trash rack panel 4, at elevation 218.4 metres, inside a sealed concrete gallery.

There are three ways into that gallery and two of them were shut. The inspection hatch was dogged from the outside at 16:40 on the eighteenth and not opened until the recovery team opened it at 07:25. The draft tube ran under vacuum all night, outward and downward. The stoplogs were in.

The only route from the forebay to panel 4 is through intake gate 2, while gate 2 is open and flowing. With the gate shut, the gallery is dry above the sill at 211 metres — and she was seven metres above it.

Intake gate 2 was open 02:31:08 to 03:12:44. Nothing else, until 08:40.

Ines Ballard entered the water between 02:31 and 03:12, and she has been on that rack ever since.

And if you bought the rack trace, you know it twice over: the debris mat that formed at the 04:12 inflow peak lay over her.

Evidence: D-2, D-2A, D-2B, D-1 §2, and SLIP 04.

Step 3 · A card is not a person

Her credential opened cabinet C-14 at 05:47 and closed the F-3 tie at 06:02. She had been dead for about three hours.

Her card was recovered at 10:22 from the second drawer of the control room desk. Not on her body. Not in her locker. In a drawer, 260 metres and two levels from where she was found — and it was on the property schedule the whole time, at line 31, where nobody looked at it because by then everybody knew what had happened.

Standing Order 11.4 says responsibility for a recorded event rests with the holder. That is a rule about discipline. It was read as a finding of fact, and it is not one.

Evidence: SLIP 02, C-5A, E-1A.

Step 4 · Why a correct isolation killed him

Aurelio Sarn did everything right.

He isolated feeder F-5, applied his own padlock, hung his tag, and proved the circuit dead with a tester this department has since checked and found accurate. The cabinet was dead when he opened it.

It was fed by a second circuit. Feeder F-3 — certified permanently decommissioned and physically removed in 2019, under contract RG-0419, and never touched. Still there. Still live. Still switchable from the control room. And erased from the as-built drawing at revision C in 2019 by the same man who signed the certificate.

The drawing in the control room — the one an engineer reads before he isolates — showed C-14 as single-fed. It was that drawing that killed him, three and a half years after it was amended.

Evidence: E-6, SLIP 07, E-2A.

Step 5 · Who could have used her card

The control room door did not open between 02:29:55 and 05:44:03.

Three hours and fourteen minutes, one door, badge-controlled both ways, fire escape alarmed and silent. Halloran Vey went in at 21:57 and did not come out. Nobody else went in. Her lanyard was in the desk.

And the F-3 tie was not closed from some anonymous terminal. The log says where: console 1.

Evidence: E-1, D-1 §1, D-1A.

Step 6 · The twenty-six minutes

His alibi was true, and total, and that is why it took a review to break it.

He logged on to console 1 at 21:58 and off at 07:41 — a single unbroken session of nine hours and forty-three minutes, no lock events, no second operator. Every record on the site says Halloran Vey was at his desk.

Except the one that actually checks. Storm-mode alarms escalate after ninety seconds unacknowledged. Across the entire night there is one gap:

02:38 to 03:04. Eleven alarms escalate, unanswered.

At 03:05:12, all eleven are cleared in thirty-eight seconds — the rate of a man sitting back down.

Control room to forebay parapet: four minutes. There and back with eighteen minutes to spare.

He opened gate 2 at 02:31. He stood up at 02:38. She went into the water at about 02:50 and the gate he had opened carried her inside. He sat down at 03:04, cleared his screen, and shut the gate at 03:12.

A session log records that a session is open. Only the alarms record whether anybody is in the chair.

Evidence: SLIP 01, against his own account at C-1 and the session record at D-1A.

Ines Ballard was in the water. (D-2A, D-1 §2)

Step 8 · Why

$186,000, through a company registered to his wife, from the only client it ever had.

Contract RG-0419 had two scopes. Install six foundation piezometers. Remove one legacy feeder. Thennick Instrumentation delivered a junction box and some cable, invoiced $1.41 million, and did neither. Halloran Vey certified both complete, and within nine days of each certification Ashgrove Technical Services invoiced Thennick for advisory work of which no trace has ever been produced.

For five years he generated the readings himself. The dam's actual foundation pore pressures are unknown and have been unknown since 2019, and the station is now under a Section 10 direction while somebody finds out.

At half past one in the morning, in a storm, Ines Ballard noticed that six numbers were not moving with a reservoir that had come up a third of a metre.

Evidence: SLIP 06, SLIP 11, E-2, E-2A.

Double Unlawful Killing

HALLORAN VEY

Ines Ballard is exonerated.

What happened

They went down forty-one steps at ten past two and opened a junction box and there was nothing in it. At twenty-five past, Sarn told him it was going to the regulator at first light — and the young man across the river heard two angry voices over a keyed radio and assumed, because it was two in the morning, that the second one was hers.

At 02:31 he opened a gate. It was a defensible operation and the Inspectorate has confirmed as much; passing water at 126 cumecs before a peak is exactly what the manual says. It was also a route out of the valley that runs through a concrete gallery, and he knew it, because he had spent eleven years watching that station take in everything that floated.

At 02:38 he stood up. She was crossing the crest to the site office for the as-built drawings — the drawings he had amended in 2019 — and he met her on the way.

Then he came back, cleared eleven alarms in thirty-eight seconds, shut the gate, and worked through the rest of the storm. At twenty to six he told a man he had known for a decade that there was an earth fault on F-5, and watched him go and do everything correctly.

He was undone by an alarm system counting to ninety.
