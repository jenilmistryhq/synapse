Console 1 · Alarm Acknowledgement Log

Alarm Acknowledgement — 18–19 November

How this log works

In storm mode every alarm at console 1 must be acknowledged by the operator within ninety seconds. Unacknowledged, it escalates to the auto-dialler and wakes somebody at home. Acknowledgement is a physical keypress at the console. The log is therefore a continuous record of whether a human being is in the chair.

Cairnhold Power — Civil Engineering
Trash Rack Differential-Pressure Trace

When the Debris Arrived

Differential pressure across the intake trash rack is monitored continuously. Debris loading obstructs flow and the differential rises. The trace is a direct record of what is sitting on the rack and when it got there.

Trace — 19 November

TimeDifferential (kPa)Interpretation

04:123.8STEP CHANGE. Peak inflow overtops into the gallery approach; a debris mat consolidates across panels 3–5.
05:004.1Mat compacting.
07:004.1Static.

Composition of the Mat

Bracken, spruce needles, birch twig, and one length of larch fence rail bearing a Ravensgate Estate stamp. The estate reported the loss of approximately eleven metres of boundary fencing from the north shore during the peak of the storm, between 04:00 and 05:00. The material in the mat is that fencing and what came down the hill with it.

The mat formed at 04:12. The deceased was underneath it.

She was against panel 4 before 04:12. Since the only flow into the gallery that night ceased at 03:12, and the racks were raked clear on 17 November, she reached the rack during the single period of flow and nothing has moved her since.
