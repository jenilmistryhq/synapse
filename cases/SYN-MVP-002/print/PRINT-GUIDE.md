# PRINT GUIDE — SYN-MVP-002 "The Ravensgate Interlock"

**HARD tier. 53 sheets, 17 envelopes.** Larger than Case 01 because the file is split five ways —
that split *is* the case.

## 4 · AUTHORITY RESULTS — pages 1–12, single-sided ⚠️ SPOILERS

**Page number = Authority number.** Page 1 is Authority 01, page 12 is Authority 12.

Collect the stack face-down out of the printer and sleeve it without reading. Twelve envelopes
marked `01`–`12`.

Four of the twelve return nothing new. That is deliberate and the players are told so — telling
them apart *before* paying is the skill.

## TOTALS

| Setup | Sheets | Envelopes |
|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | 53 | 16 |
| **2–4 detectives** | 55 | 16 |
| **5–6 detectives** | 56 | 16 |

**Envelopes: 16.** Twelve numbered `01`–`12`, four marked RECONSIDER, plus one for S-1 = 17 if you
count S-1 separately, which you should. The five Specialist File bundles need clips, not envelopes.

### Reading load

**11,163 player-facing words** — roughly 56 minutes of reading, but split five ways it is about
**11 minutes each** in Session 1. That is the point of the split, and it is why this case takes
120–160 minutes rather than 90.

### Minimum-ink variant — 38 sheets

- Case Board double-sided → 4 sheets, no spares.
- Specialist Files double-sided **within each bundle only** → 10 sheets. Do not let a bundle
  boundary fall mid-sheet.
- Play materials double-sided → 3 sheets.
- Authority results and Reconsider inserts **must stay single-sided**.
- S-1 double-sided → 3 sheets.
